Welcome to the FitSquad Hub!

FitSquad Hub is a community fitness platform where squad members can participate in fun workout challenges and stay motivated together

Members can track the duration of their workouts, earn XP reward points, which can later be redeemed through partner companies for supplements and sports gear, and participate in challenges across various categories and difficulty levels, ranging from Beginner to Advanced

FitSquad Hub is designed to make fitness more engaging and social by encouraging members to stay active, connect with the community, and build consistent exercise habits
